  <section class="experiment-section">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">June 23, 2026</p><h3>DNA extraction and inverse Phusion PCR</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-layout"><article class="experiment-section-copy">
      <p><strong>DNA extraction:</strong> Plasmid MH45 was extracted from <em>E. coli</em> Ek317 with the QIA Spin MiniPrep workflow. A 1–5 ml overnight culture was pelleted at 9,000 rpm for 10 minutes at 25 °C. The pellet was processed with 250 µl P1, 250 µl P2, and 350 µl N3, clarified at 13,000 rpm, loaded onto a QIAprep 2.0 column, washed with 0.5 ml PB and 0.75 ml PE, dry-spun for 3 minutes, heated at 55 °C for 3 minutes, and eluted with 30 µl ddH<sub>2</sub>O.</p>
      <p><strong>Inverse PCR:</strong> VISP1 and VISP2 were used to amplify the plasmid outward, linearize the vector, and add restriction-enzyme binding sites. The template volume calculation was 20 ng ÷ 40.67 ng/µl ≈ 0.49 µl.</p>
      <p><strong>Thermocycle:</strong> 98 °C for 30 s; 30 cycles of 98 °C for 10 s, 61 °C for 20 s, and 72 °C for 1 min 45 s; 72 °C for 3 min; 4 °C hold.</p>
    </article><div class="experiment-figure-slot experiment-figure-slot-wide"><span>30 µl reaction</span><small>20 ng template · 10 mM dNTP · VISP1/VISP2 · Phusion polymerase</small></div></div>
  </section>

  <section class="experiment-section experiment-section-reverse">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">June 25, 2026</p><h3>DNA cleanup, DpnI digestion, and MT amplification</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-layout"><div class="experiment-figure-slot experiment-figure-slot-wide"><span>MT primer plan</span><small>MT1: 3+4 · MT2: 3+5 · MT3: 3+6 · MT4: 3+7</small></div><article class="experiment-section-copy">
      <p>PCR products were purified on Zymo columns using a 1:2 DNA-to-binding-buffer ratio. Columns were washed twice, dry-spun at 14,000 rpm for 3 minutes, heated at 55 °C, and eluted with 20 µl ddH<sub>2</sub>O.</p>
      <p>The vector was digested with DpnI at 37 °C for 2 hours to remove methylated parental plasmid. Lyophilized primers 3–7 were reconstituted, and four MT inserts were amplified on ice. MT1 used 59.8 °C annealing; MT2, MT3, and MT4 used 60.8 °C.</p>
      <p>Purified vector and MT products were double-digested with XbaI and SalI for 2 hours at 37 °C.</p>
    </article></div>
  </section>

  <section class="experiment-section">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">June 29, 2026</p><h3>Ligation and bacterial cultivation</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-layout"><article class="experiment-section-copy">
      <p>Vector cleanup used a 1:2 DNA-to-binding-buffer ratio; MT PCR products used 1:5. MT1, MT3, and MT4 were ligated to vector MH45 using 100 ng vector and a 3:1 insert-to-vector molar ratio. MT2 was deferred because its recorded concentration was low.</p>
      <ul class="experiment-list"><li><strong>Vector:</strong> 100 ng ÷ 42.9 = 2.33 µl</li><li><strong>MT1:</strong> concentration 29.3; insert 1.62 µl; ddH<sub>2</sub>O 13.85 µl</li><li><strong>MT3:</strong> concentration 22.7; insert 2.11 µl; ddH<sub>2</sub>O 13.36 µl</li><li><strong>MT4:</strong> concentration 39.2; insert 0.82 µl; ddH<sub>2</sub>O 14.65 µl</li></ul>
      <p><em>E. coli</em> Ek317 was cultivated in 2 ml SOB at 37 °C and 225 rpm.</p>
    </article><div class="experiment-figure-slot experiment-figure-slot-wide"><span>Ligation program</span><small>16 °C for 14 hr · 37 °C for 1 hr · 4 °C hold</small></div></div>
  </section>

  <section class="experiment-section experiment-section-reverse">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">June 30, 2026</p><h3>MT2 PCR, competent cells, and electroporation</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-layout"><div class="experiment-figure-slot experiment-figure-slot-wide"><span>Transformation</span><small>4 µl construct + 40 µl competent cells · 800 µl SOC recovery</small></div><article class="experiment-section-copy">
      <p>MT2 was re-amplified with primers 3 and 5 using a 60.8 °C annealing temperature. An overnight Ek317 culture was subcultured into 50 ml SOB and grown to approximately OD<sub>600</sub> 0.6.</p>
      <p>Cells were chilled, centrifuged at 9,000 rpm and 4 °C, repeatedly washed with cold ddH<sub>2</sub>O, concentrated to 200 µl, and aliquoted at 40 µl per tube.</p>
      <p>MT1, MT3, and MT4 ligation products were electroporated. Cells recovered in warm SOC for one hour at 37 °C and 225 rpm, then undiluted and 10<sup>−1</sup> samples were plated on LB + kanamycin.</p>
    </article></div>
  </section>

  <section class="experiment-section">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">July 2, 2026</p><h3>MT1 colony PCR and MT2 digestion</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-layout"><article class="experiment-section-copy">
      <p>Sixteen MT1 colonies were transferred to a mapped grid plate. Each colony was resuspended in 10 µl sterile ddH<sub>2</sub>O and heated at 96 °C for 12 minutes.</p>
      <p>Colony PCR used a 14 µl Taq reaction and the following program: 94 °C for 2 minutes; 35 cycles of 94 °C for 30 seconds, 57.6 °C for 10 seconds, and 72 °C for 2 minutes; 72 °C for 3 minutes 30 seconds; 4 °C hold.</p>
      <p>MT2 was double-digested with XbaI and SalI. Sixteen MT3 colonies and sixteen MT4 colonies were also picked onto grid plates.</p>
    </article><div class="experiment-figure-slot experiment-figure-slot-wide"><span>Screening scale</span><small>16 colonies per construct on mapped grid plates</small></div></div>
  </section>
