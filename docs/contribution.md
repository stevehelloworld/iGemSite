    <p class="experiment-lead">This page shares the practical record produced by our 2026 cadmium-response project: a paired construct design, a plate-reader workflow, and the limits we encountered when interpreting GFP as a proxy for cadmium availability.</p>

  <section class="experiment-section">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">What is shared</p><h3>Design and experimental records</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-copy">
      <p>The <a href="parts">Parts</a> page documents four MT candidates and their paired Lpp-OmpA anchor-present and anchor-deleted designs. The <a href="methods">Experiments</a> page records cloning, sequence-checking, and plate-reader procedures. The <a href="notebook">Notebook</a> preserves dated build and troubleshooting notes, while <a href="results">Results</a> reports growth and GFP measurements across six cadmium concentrations.</p>
      <p>This record may help another team plan a comparison between surface-display and intracellular protein designs, keep construct identity linked to plate labels, and anticipate the need for matched controls and sequence checks.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="experiment-section experiment-section-reverse">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">Interpretation lesson</p><h3>Reporter suppression is not a direct removal measurement</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-layout">
      <div class="experiment-figure-slot experiment-figure-slot-wide"><span>Key limitation</span><small>GFP and OD<sub>600</sub> do not quantify cadmium associated with cells or left in solution</small></div>
      <article class="experiment-section-copy">
        <p>Our data show construct-dependent changes in GFP and growth, but they do not measure cadmium in cell pellets or culture supernatant. Lower GFP can be consistent with reduced cadmium reaching the sensor, but it can also arise from altered expression, growth, or cell physiology. We therefore share both the observed signals and the additional tests needed before making a cadmium-binding or removal claim.</p>
        <p>Future teams can use this distinction when choosing an assay: pair a reporter with direct metal quantification, a constitutive expression control, and a test of protein localization.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="experiment-section">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">Scope</p><h3>What this contribution does not claim</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-copy">
      <ul>
        <li>We do not claim that the project established a validated cadmium-removal process.</li>
        <li>We do not claim that the measured reporter signal directly quantifies cadmium binding.</li>
        <li>We do not claim a new Registry part on this page; any part status should be checked against the current iGEM Registry record.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>The contribution is the transparent, reusable documentation of our design and measurements, including the uncertainty that remains.</p>
