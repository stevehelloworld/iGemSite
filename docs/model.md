    <article class="model-card model-card-accent">
      <p class="model-card-kicker">What we model</p>
      <h3>Turning a biological system into a measurable framework</h3>
      <p>
        Our model can describe how key variables interact, how a design
        decision changes the predicted outcome, and where the experimental
        bottlenecks may appear before we commit to a lab build.
      </p>
      <p>
        This helps us make the engineering cycle more efficient and keeps the
        computational work tied to real experimental goals.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article class="model-card">
      <p class="model-card-kicker">Why it matters</p>
      <h3>Using predictions to guide the next build</h3>
      <p>
        Simulation gives us a way to compare hypotheses quickly, choose better
        parameter ranges, and interpret results with more confidence.
      </p>
      <p>
        The best iGEM model pages usually do one thing very well: they show
        clearly how the model changes what the team does next. That is the main
        visual direction we are following here.
