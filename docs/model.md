    <h2>Exploratory metallothionein structure predictions</h2>
    <p class="experiment-lead">This page describes the computational material currently shown on the wiki: predicted structures and Predicted Aligned Error plots for the four MT fusion constructs. These are exploratory predictions, not a mathematical model of cadmium removal and not experimental confirmation of protein folding, surface display, or cadmium binding.</p>
  </div>

  <section class="experiment-section">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">What is available</p><h3>Four predicted construct structures</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-copy">
      <p>The <a href="methods#mt-structure-title">Experiments page</a> displays predicted structures and PAE plots for IaMT2, SmtA, EGR_09832, and OsMTI-1b constructs. The PAE plots describe model uncertainty in relative residue placement; they do not measure protein abundance, localization, or metal-binding performance in our cells.</p>
      <p>The documents currently published on this wiki do not record enough model provenance to reproduce the predictions, including the exact service or software version, input sequence files, and run settings. Those details should be added if the team can verify them from the original modeling files.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="experiment-section experiment-section-reverse">
    <div class="experiment-section-header"><p class="experiment-card-kicker">How to read it</p><h3>Predictions guide questions; assays test them</h3></div>
    <div class="experiment-section-layout">
      <div class="experiment-figure-slot experiment-figure-slot-wide"><span>Prediction ≠ validation</span><small>Structure models do not demonstrate cadmium capture or removal</small></div>
      <article class="experiment-section-copy">
        <p>We used the predicted structures as a visual reference for the selected MT constructs. The current wet-lab experiments measured cell growth and GFP fluorescence. They did not test whether a predicted structure formed, whether the MT reached the intended cellular location, or how much cadmium it bound.</p>
        <p>A future analysis could compare predicted features with verified protein expression and direct cadmium measurements. No quantitative cadmium-removal simulation or prediction is documented here.</p>
