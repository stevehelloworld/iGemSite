    <span class="problem-eyebrow">Background</span>
    <h1>Heavy metal contamination threatens water safety, agriculture, and public health.</h1>
    <p>
      Industrial wastewater can carry toxic metal ions into rivers, soil, and irrigation systems.
      Once released, these pollutants are difficult to remove and may accumulate through the food
      chain, creating long-term environmental risks.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="problem-content-grid">
    <article class="problem-text-card">
      <h2>Why heavy metals matter</h2>
      <p>
        Lead and cadmium pollution are especially concerning because both metals can persist in the
        environment and cause harm even at low concentrations. Their movement through wastewater,
        soil, crops, and ecosystems makes early detection and effective remediation essential.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article class="problem-text-card">
      <h2>Why wastewater is the focus</h2>
      <p>
        Wastewater is one of the key pathways by which industrial pollution reaches natural and
        agricultural systems. By targeting contamination before it spreads, our project aims to
        support cleaner water and safer environments.
      </p>
    </article>
  </div>
